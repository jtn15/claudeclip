Our Story

Maison Rivault makes quiet, well-made knitwear for men who want to look put-together without trying, built in layers you can wear from the first cold morning to the last. Est. 2026. [confirm: location, e.g. Phoenix, Arizona]

Maison Rivault begins with a narrow idea: a small set of knitwear, in four colors, that goes together without effort. Navy, camel, olive and cream. Pieces that sit over and under each other, so a cold morning and a mild afternoon call for the same few things.

Drop 001 is six styles: a quarter-zip, a knit polo, a crewneck, an overshirt, a scarf and a beanie. Each comes in one color, chosen to work with the rest. There are no seasonal reinventions and no long catalog to sort through.

We are a new brand, and we would rather say so than borrow a history. What we can offer is a short list of well-chosen pieces, described plainly, with fabric, fit and care stated on every page. Drop 002, with a cardigan, trousers and footwear, comes next.
