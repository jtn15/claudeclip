# Shipping and returns

## Returns

**You have 30 days.** You can return any eligible item within 30 days of the delivery date shown on your carrier's tracking.

**What can be returned.** Items must be:

- unworn, unwashed and unaltered
- in original condition with all tags attached
- in the original packaging

**What can't be returned** (unless it arrived defective or not as described):

- leather accessories
- personalized, monogrammed or made-to-order items
- gift cards and store credit

**How to start a return.**

1. Email **info@maisonrivault.com** within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number, the items you're returning and the reason.
2. Within 2 business days we'll reply with a return authorization and the address to send it to.
3. Pack the item and send it to the address we give you. Please don't send it to the address on your shipping label or packaging, because returns sent there can get lost.

**Who pays return shipping.** We do, on standard returns (change of mind, fit or style). We'll send you a tracked, insured label. **When you get your refund.** We inspect your return once it arrives and email you the result. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Allow 5 to 10 business days after approval for it to show up, depending on your bank. Original shipping charges aren't refunded unless the item was defective or we sent the wrong thing.

**Prefer store credit?** Choose store credit instead of a refund and we'll add a 10% bonus, so you get 110% of the refund value as a gift card code. Store credit doesn't expire and can't be exchanged for cash.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, email **info@maisonrivault.com** within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the problem. For damage in transit, also include photos of the packaging and the shipping label. Once we confirm it, we'll send a replacement at no cost or give you a full refund that includes your original shipping, whichever you choose.
