# Returns and refunds

Thanks for shopping with Maison Rivault. If something isn't right, here's how we'll fix it.

## 30 days to decide

You can return items within **30 days** of the delivery date on your tracking. To be returnable, an item needs to be unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with all tags attached, and in its original packaging (including any Maison Rivault extras that came with it).

A few things can't be returned unless they arrived defective or not as described:

- leather accessories
- personalized, monogrammed or made-to-order items
- gift cards and store credit

## How to return something

1. Email **info@maisonrivault.com** with your order number, what you're returning and why.
2. Within 2 business days we'll reply with a return authorization and the return address.
3. Send your item to that address. Please don't use the address on your shipping label, because returns sent there can get lost. Emailing us first is how we make sure your return reaches us.

**Return shipping is on us** for standard returns (change of mind, fit or style). We'll use a tracked, insured service. We can refund your return once it reaches us.

## Free size exchanges

Need a different size? Exchanges for size are free, and we cover shipping both ways. Email us with your order number and the size you want, and we'll check stock. For a different style or color, return the original and place a new order. That's the quickest way to get the piece before it sells out.

## Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, defective or isn't what you ordered, email **info@maisonrivault.com** within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue. For damage in transit, include photos of the packaging and shipping label too.

Once we confirm the problem, you choose:

- a replacement at no cost to you, or
- a full refund, including your original shipping.

We'll cover any return shipping we need, and sometimes we'll just ask you to keep or recycle the item.

## Your refund

We'll inspect your return and email you once it's approved. You can choose:

- **Refund to your original payment method.** Allow 5 to 10 business days after approval for it to appear, depending on your bank.
- **Store credit with a 10% bonus.** You get 110% of your refund value as a gift card code. It doesn't expire (except where the law requires) and can't be exchanged for cash or transferred.

You choose when your return is approved, and the choice is final once it's issued. Original shipping charges aren't refunded, except for damaged, defective or wrong items. Taxes on returned items are refunded as the law requires.

If an item comes back worn, washed, damaged or without its tags, we can't accept it. We'll let you know and can send it back to you.

## Bundles

Bundles are priced as a set. If you return part of a bundle, the bundle discount no longer applies to what you keep. Your refund is what you paid, minus the regular price of the items you keep. For example, if two $100 items cost $180 as a bundle and you return one, your refund is $80 ($180 minus $100). Return the whole bundle and you get the full bundle price back.

## Changing or canceling an order

Orders are packed quickly. To cancel or change your address, email **info@maisonrivault.com** within 12 hours of ordering. After that we may not be able to stop it, but you can still return it under this policy once it arrives.

## Something wrong? Talk to us first

If there's a problem with your order, email **info@maisonrivault.com** and we'll work it out with you, usually within 2 business days. Shipping questions (delivery times, lost or delayed packages, international duties) are covered in our Shipping Policy.

## The fine print

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under consumer protection law. We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect when you placed your order is the one that applies to it.
